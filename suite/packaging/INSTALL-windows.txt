LGN MIXING SUITE FOR WINDOWS (VST3)

EQ, Compressor, De-Esser, Saturator, Reverb, Delay, Limiter, Gravity, Duck, Distance, Era, Swing, Mood and Flipside (effects), plus three instruments: Haze (the flagship mono bass synth), Strike (a drum synthesizer) and Fret (a modelled guitar).
Duck replaces the older LGN Duck download: same plugin, so your saved projects keep their settings.

1. Copy every .vst3 folder to  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
2. In FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find more plugins.
3. The effects appear under Effects as "LGN EQ", "LGN Compressor" and so on. LGN Haze, LGN Strike and LGN Fret appear under Generators.

Presets: click the preset name at the top of any plugin for the factory presets, or use the
< > arrows to step through them. They also appear in your DAW's own preset menu.

If Windows SmartScreen warns about them, choose "More info" > "Run anyway" (the plugins aren't code-signed).
