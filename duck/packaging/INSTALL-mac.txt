LGN DUCK FOR MAC (VST3 + AU)

Easiest: double-click install-mac.command (it copies both versions into place and clears the
"downloaded from the internet" flag so the Mac lets the plugin load).

Manual: copy "LGN Duck.vst3" to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/ and "LGN Duck.component" to
~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/, then rescan plugins in your DAW.

TEMPO mode (default): press play. It pumps in time with your song. No routing needed.
KICK mode: send your kick to LGN Duck's sidechain input, then click KICK.
